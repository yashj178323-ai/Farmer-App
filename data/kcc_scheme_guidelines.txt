Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme Rules & Guidelines:
1. Eligibility: All farmers, individual/joint borrowers, tenant farmers, sharecroppers, and Self Help Groups (SHGs) are eligible for KCC.
2. Loan Limits & Interest Subvention: The government provides an interest subvention of 2% per annum for short-term crop loans up to Rs. 3 Lakhs. An additional 3% prompt repayment incentive (PRI) is given to farmers who pay on time, reducing the effective interest rate to 4% per annum.
3. Collateral Waiver: Collateral security is waived for KCC agricultural credit loans up to Rs. 1.60 Lakhs (extended up to Rs. 2.00 Lakhs for tie-up loans).
4. Validity & Processing Fee: The KCC card is valid for 5 years with annual review. Processing fees are waived for KCC loans up to Rs. 3 Lakhs.
