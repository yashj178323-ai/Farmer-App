PACS (Primary Agricultural Credit Societies) Model Bylaws:
1. Membership Criteria: Any farmer, rural artisan, or laborer residing within the operational area of the PACS is eligible for A-class membership with voting rights upon purchasing a minimum of 1 share.
2. Crop Loan Disbursement: Scale of Finance (SoF) determined by District Level Technical Committee (DLTC) governs maximum short-term credit allowed per acre for specified crops.
3. Non-Agricultural Credit: PACS can provide short-term credit for allied activities like dairy, poultry, fisheries, and micro-enterprises up to 20% of total loan portfolio.
